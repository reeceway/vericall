# VeriCall SaaS Business Model

## 🎯 Target Market

**Primary: Enterprise B2B**
- Banks & Financial Services (prevent wire fraud)
- Insurance companies (claim verification)
- Healthcare (HIPAA-compliant patient verification)
- Crypto/Web3 (high-value transaction confirmation)
- Government (secure communications)

**Why B2B?**
- High willingness to pay for security
- Regulatory compliance needs
- Large transaction values justify cost
- Long sales cycles but sticky customers

---

## 💰 Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Developer ($0 - Freemium)
- 100 calls/month
- Basic device verification
- Community support
- **Goal:** Get developers building, word-of-mouth

### Tier 2: Growth ($499/month)
- 10,000 calls/month
- Device + Voice verification
- Real-time dashboard
- Email support
- **Target:** Startups, small fintechs

### Tier 3: Business ($2,499/month)
- 100,000 calls/month
- Advanced analytics
- Custom voice thresholds
- SLA: 99.9% uptime
- Priority support
- **Target:** Mid-size banks, insurers

### Tier 4: Enterprise (Custom - $10K+/month)
- Unlimited calls
- On-premise deployment option
- Custom ML models
- Dedicated success manager
- SOC 2 compliance
- **Target:** Major banks, healthcare systems

## 📊 Value Metrics

**Primary:** Per-verified-call
- Charge per call where verification occurs
- Not per API call (too granular)
- Range: $0.05 - $0.50 per call

**Secondary:** Per-seat (for admin dashboard)
- $50-200/month per admin user
- Unlimited viewing, limited verification calls

**Why this works:**
- Customers pay for value received (fraud prevented)
- Scales with usage (grows with customer)
- Easy to understand ROI

---

## 🏆 Value Proposition Math

**For a bank:**
- Average wire fraud loss: $50,000
- VeriCall prevents: 10 fraud attempts/month
- Value created: $500,000/month
- VeriCall cost: $2,499/month
- **ROI: 200x**

**For insurance:**
- Fake claim payout: $25,000
- VeriCall verifies: 50 claims/month
- Value: $1.25M in prevented fraud
- Cost: $2,499/month
- **ROI: 500x**

**Pricing psychology:** Charge 0.1% of value delivered

## 📈 Unit Economics

**Cost per verification:**
- Cloud compute: $0.001
- Storage: $0.0001
- Bandwidth: $0.001
- **Total cost: ~$0.002 per call**

**Pricing: $0.05 - $0.50 per call**

**Gross margin: 95%+**

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):**
- Enterprise sales cycle: 6 months
- Sales team cost: $150K/year per rep
- Marketing: $50K/month
- **CAC: ~$20K per enterprise customer**

**LTV (Lifetime Value):**
- Average contract: $24K/year
- Churn: 5% annually
- Lifetime: 20 years
- **LTV: $480K**

**LTV/CAC ratio: 24:1** (Excellent)
