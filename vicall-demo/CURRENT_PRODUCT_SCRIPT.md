# Vicall Demo Script — Current Product Safe Version

This version keeps the emotional structure of the draft, but it matches the current product more closely.

## Core corrections

- Use `Vicall`, not `ViCall`.
- Do not say `nothing ever leaves the phone`.
- Do not say `no cloud processing` as a blanket statement.
- Do not say `under a second` for clone detection.
- Do not say `every call is verified instantly`.

## Safer replacements

### Scene 4

Instead of:

> On-device. Nothing ever leaves the phone.

Use:

> The detection decision runs on-device, so the alerting logic stays local to the phone.

### Scene 6

Instead of:

> The AI confirmed the caller is real, in under a second.

Use:

> After a few seconds of live speech, the app can show a green human-voice signal on the call.

### Scene 7

Instead of:

> Vicall catches it in under one second.

Use:

> During the call, Vicall can flag synthetic-sounding audio and escalate the warning on screen.

### Scene 8

Instead of:

> Everything runs on your device. No audio is recorded. No voice data leaves the phone. No cloud.

Use:

> Detection runs on-device. Vicall does not store call recordings. The alerting decision does not require cloud inference.

## Recommended voiceover copy

### Scene 4

> Vicall is a secure calling app deployed by your IT team. It gives your employees a protected line with on-device detection that monitors live calls for synthetic audio. Your team gets one link, installs the app, and starts using it.

### Scene 5

> Making a secure call works like a normal call. Open the app, tap a contact, and call. The detection runs quietly in the background while the conversation happens.

### Scene 6

> When an employee receives a call, it feels familiar. They answer like they always do. Then, after enough live speech is available, the app can show a green human-voice signal right on the call screen.

### Scene 7

> If the audio starts to look synthetic, the chip turns red and the alert becomes impossible to ignore. The warning appears while the call is still live, before the situation turns into damage.

### Scene 8

> The detection decision runs on the device. Vicall does not store call recordings, and the alerting path does not depend on cloud inference.
