# VeriCall Consumer Business Model

## 🎯 Consumer Use Cases

### 1. Scam Protection (Primary)
**Problem:** Consumers lose $10B/year to phone scams (IRS, tech support, grandparent scams)
**Solution:** Verify caller is who they claim before answering

### 2. Family Safety
- Verify it's really your kid calling from school
- Confirm elderly parent's voice (detect if someone else has their phone)
- "Safe list" - only verified family calls ring through

### 3. Dating Safety
- Verify dating app matches before meeting
- Voice verification = real person, not catfish
- Share verification badge on profile

### 4. Marketplace Trust
- Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist
- Verify seller/buyer before transaction
- "Verified caller" badge builds trust

### 5. Professional Networking
- LinkedIn calls
- Verify recruiter, potential employer
- Cold calls with credibility

## 📊 Consumer Economics

**Cost per user:**
- Infrastructure: $0.10/month
- Support: $0.50/month
- Marketing: $3/month (paid acquisition)
- **Total: $3.60/month**

**Revenue per user:**
- 5% convert to Premium @ $4.99 = $0.25/mo avg
- Plus Lifetime sales = $0.10/mo avg
- **Total: $0.35/month**

**Problem:** Losing money on free users

**Solution:**
- Ad-supported free tier ($0.50/mo per user)
- Limit free tier to 20 calls (drive conversion)
- Focus on organic growth (lower CAC)

**Break-even:** 10% conversion rate
